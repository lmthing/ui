# Google Play Best of: what transfers from standout apps

These are US editorial awards, not a test of Material adoption. Evidence below is what Google or the makers publish about the apps. The design readings and animation proposals are ours; we did not install the apps or measure their actual motion, accessibility or performance. The November 2026 winners are the current reference, with two contrasting 2025 examples.

## Evidence and design readings

| Award and app                                                | Published behavior                                                                                                                    | Transferable design pattern                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2026 Best App and Best Productivity Partner: Notability**  | Native Android digital notebook; capture and organize notes, work from PDFs, and turn material into questions, flashcards or quizzes. | Keep the user's notebook as the primary object. Put interpretation near the material, not in a disconnected AI hub. On compact, returning from a quiz should reopen the originating context, not a reset library. |
| **2026 Best Cross-device App: Strava**                       | Record on Wear OS; see weekly progress at a glance on a lock-screen widget; explore activities on phones, foldables and tablets.      | Give each surface the part of the job it does best: capture, glance, or analyze. Keep activity identity and status consistent rather than copying a full phone dashboard onto the watch.                          |
| **2026 Best Innovative App: Wispr Flow**                     | Dictation converts spoken words to text in the field in use; Android and desktop use different entry points.                          | A new input mode should land in an ordinary, editable draft. Make listening, processing and editable result legible; never require voice or automatically send a result just because transcription finished.      |
| **2026 Best for Entertainment: Qobuz**                       | Combines playback with human-curated playlists, interviews and music reviews.                                                         | A product can be recognizable through editorial selection and type/imagery, not extra animation. Keep the current playback state when moving from an article to a record and back.                                |
| **2026 Best for Large Screens: BandLab**                     | A music-making studio with production tools and a sound library; Play names its large-screen experience separately.                   | As more space opens, give the supporting tools their own room without replacing the central creative task. In a 390-wide treatment prioritize the current track and transport, and reveal extra work in context.  |
| **2025 Best App and Best for Personal Growth: Focus Friend** | A focus timer built around a small companion and a reason to finish a session.                                                        | Pair a repeated utility with one clear emotional hook and meaningful completion. Treat the live countdown as the functional core; don't bury Start, Stop or remaining time behind character interactions.         |
| **2025 Best Multi-device App: Luminar**                      | Photo editing experience recognized for use across devices.                                                                           | Keep the photo and its edits as the shared record. Bring the newest state and the right control density to each form factor; a view transition must not look like it applied an edit.                             |

## Applying the lessons at 390

1. **Make a core loop visible without explanation.** In a focus screen, show one timer value, one main action and readable completion context; give completion one cue, and don't animate when waiting in the idle state.
2. **Keep identity through state changes.** In a notebook or activity list, motion connects the chosen item to its detail only if both have stable identities; retain list filters, draft or playhead on return. Offer reduced equivalent.
3. **Put new capabilities inside an established task.** Dictation inserts a draft; analysis appears next to the subject; quieter controls remain visible. Keep failure and undo honest.
4. **Treat adaptation as role selection.** Use a single readable pane before a miniature workspace; then reveal a second pane on larger windows. Test handoff, state restoration and insets on actual host platforms. Google Play's technical quality criteria are separate evidence from awards and include navigation, accessibility, stability and form-factor checks.
5. **Review the craft, not the screenshot effect.** Look for immediate feedback, consistent destination state, clean expensive transitions and a pause/cancel path. Follow Android system back on native; on web preserve URL/history, focus and keyboard access.

### For lmthing

Use `Timer`/`Streak` for the Focus Friend lesson, `FileList`/`Composer`/`Choice` for document-to-task, `Route`/`Progress`/`Stats` for the activity loop, and `Player`/`Article`/`Gallery` for editorial playback. Studio, media, learning, sports and mind packs can differ by their primary job, imagery and one signature, all using closed choices. A candidate review check: ask what makes this app recognizable with color removed, whether a first-time user can do its primary job, and which one motion cue teaches or rewards that job. Gap: curated end-to-end exemplar flows with captured interaction, reduced mode and continuity beyond app screenshots; store examples alone do not validate the lmthing implementation.
