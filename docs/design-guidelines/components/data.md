# Data and time

- **Anatomy:** `Stats`, `Amount`, `Bars`, `Sparkline` and `Progress` lead with value, unit, scope/period and a comparison or goal when known. `Calendar`, `Timeline`, `Counter`, `Timer`, `Streak`, `MapPins` and `Route` make time, bounds or current location/state explicit. Summarize a chart or map in words.
- **States:** Check zero separately from unavailable, stale separately from live, and pending separately from confirmed. Retain frame and last-update time when cached; keep selected days and disabled targets named. Test negative/large values, a full series and no results.
- **Variants:** Use `figures`, `labels`, `detail`, `arrange` and `emphasis` as declared. Short numbers need an exact accessible reading. Use a single hero figure for net spend and compact aligned figures for a staffing comparison; do not hide units or exceptions.
- **Motion:** Update the changed mark or value locally and anchor its label and unit. Show actual values in reduced motion and in semantic reads. Only play an eligible signature for a meaningful update, never every timer tick or tracker poll.
- **Do / don't:** Right-align comparable figures and label trend direction independently of color. Never draw a speculative percent or animate from zero each time a dashboard opens.
