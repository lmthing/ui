# Beyond the phone

- **Anatomy:** `Camera` shows purpose, capture affordance and retake/review; `MapPins` and `Route` show current selection, scope and provenance; `Bubbles` and `People` show counterpart and role; `PassCard`, `FileList` and service actions name available offline content and external destinations.
- **States:** Check missing adapter, permission denied, offline/cached, cancel and recovery. Nothing leaves the phone until a real allowed action. Recipient acknowledgement and sender dispatch remain different states.
- **Variants:** Use map plus list alternative, declared `detail` or `trail`, and relevant media variants. Retain authority, timestamp, destination and available action at all densities.
- **Motion:** System dialogs follow the host. Keep the last record visible through a wait; never imply live location with a sweep over stale data. Reduce motion to the same readable result and offer non-gesture controls.
- **Do / don't:** Show “Last location at 10:42” and a list of stops. Do not promise a real photo upload, access grant or transfer from an illustrative sample.
