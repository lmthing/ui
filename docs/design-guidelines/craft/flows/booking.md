# Booking: make availability and commitment separate

- **Browse → select:** Show service, duration, location/resource, time zone if relevant, and dates with real availability. A date with no slots needs a reason and a next available date or waitlist where supported. Give time options a readable single-select state; make unavailable and already-selected distinct. A burst of animation every time someone changes date is wasted.
- **Review → submit:** Recap the local date/time, time zone, service, attendees, cost/deposit and cancellation terms; distinguish “Request booking” from “Confirm booking.” If a hold has an expiry, use the actual deadline and say what happens after. Back to edit restores selections. If a slot was taken or repriced, preserve the rest of the draft, explain what changed, and offer a new match. Offline cached availability is not bookable until verified.
- **Waiting → outcome:** Show “Waiting for host” or “Payment needed” with owner, next deadline if known, and cancel if safe. Confirmed has a stable ID, revised terms if any, and next actions such as add to calendar or reschedule. Declined, expired, waitlisted, cancelled, and refunded are not the same state. A reschedule does not discard the existing reservation until the change succeeds; show both times during the review.

Airbnb documents separate Instant Book and host-request paths, including identity verification, decline and expiry. Its charge timing varies; the portable lesson is to say what _this_ submission does, not to copy a deadline or claim the user hasn't been charged. On native, use the system calendar/share handoff only when eligible; on web make calendar/grid selection keyboard operable, preserve the selection across history, and offer a list or equivalent accessible view.

**For lmthing:** `book_slot` already covers availability, review, confirmation, reschedule and cancellation. `booking`'s `ServiceMenu`, `Slots`, `Availability`, `Appointment` and `Waitlist` add domain anatomy; `stays` adds `StayPolicy` and `RoomRates`. Use `KeyValue` at review, `HandoffReceipt` for another person's involvement, `Success` for confirmed, and host-owned authoritative commands. Gap: add a round-trip fixture with slot lost between review/submit, time-zone handling, pending human acceptance and a retained old booking on reschedule failure.
