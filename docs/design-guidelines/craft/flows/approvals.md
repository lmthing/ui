# Approvals: model both sides of the handoff

- **Requester drafts → submits:** Show the requested object, amount/scope, recipient/role, due date or window and optional context. A well-named request leaves a receipt with reference, status, who must act and how to amend or withdraw where possible. “Request sent” does not mean “Approved.”
- **Reviewer queue → examines:** Group by needs action versus complete; show age and urgency without relying on a red dot. Detail shows requester, impact, effective date, current record/revision, and supporting evidence. Keep Approve and Decline distinct, not two neighboring equally prominent colored buttons; require a reason when policy requires it. Use a review confirmation for unusual irreversible effects, not every routine reversible change.
- **Waiting → terminal:** Define granted, declined with permitted reason, needs changes, withdrawn, expired and already decided by someone else. Gate submissions by current role and revision, then reconcile stale results; a second reviewer sees the decision instead of reapplying it. Both sides must be able to find the final record. Keep the review context when a confirmation closes. Prefer unobtrusive waiting and a durable receipt over film-length success animation.

Airbnb's host redesign places the information used to decide near approve/decline and a real decision deadline. Apple's 2025 interactive-snippet guidance separates a request's confirmation from its result; Material separates optional inline/snackbar feedback from blocking dialog decisions. System confirms or auth surfaces are native affordances; web dialogs need an accessible heading, contained focus, a safe initial focus target and restoration on close. An OS permission is a separate decision from a teammate's approval.

**For lmthing:** `request_approve` already models two journeys and links each handoff. Compose `KeyValue`, `Note`, `HandoffReceipt`, declared `Button` confirmation or `Sheet` as appropriate, and `team`'s `Approval` or `requests`' `RequestQueue`/`RequestThread`. Let the host check role, revision, tier and settlement; make the offered actions match. Gap: cover expired, replaced, needs-changes and already-decided branches, including an approver unavailable to this requester.
