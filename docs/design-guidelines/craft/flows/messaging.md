# Messaging: own the draft, then distinguish send from delivery

- **Read:** Title the thread by person/channel and expose membership/privacy where it affects sending. Group messages under author and time anchors, with a stable unread boundary; preserve reading position when people revisit older messages. A “New messages” control can jump down without aggressively scrolling them away from what they're reading.
- **Compose:** Keep the draft per conversation when navigating away. The 390-wide composer should grow a few lines, then scroll internally; attachment chips, destination, a meaningful error and the Send action remain reachable above the keyboard. Attachments carry selected, uploading, failed, removed and ready states; don't flatten them all into a spinner.
- **Send and recover:** Distinguish queued offline, sending, accepted by service, delivered, and read only if those signals actually exist; a failed item retains content and offers safe retry/edit. Echo an optimistic entry without falsely labeling it delivered. Map pending and accepted to the same stable message once confirmed. Show edited/deleted states only where the service offers those actions; refresh can update newer content without flattening drafts.

Slack's documented draft, scheduled and sent buckets are a helpful example of states with different affordances; edit and delete are permission-dependent. On native, verify keyboard safe areas, text actions and scrolling with large type. Web must support paste, visible focus, and an explicit Send target; avoid intercepting Enter during text composition, and make multiline conventions clear. Use quiet announcements for newly available content, not a forced focus jump to every bubble or a sensory cue on each receipt.

**For lmthing:** `Bubbles`, `Composer`, `Header`, comms `Inbox`/`MailThread`/`ChannelFeed` and the `chat` archetype give the familiar surface. Typing and speech should reach the same workflow without assuming permission. Gap: model delivery-aware draft/queue/retry with a host adapter and stable public row identity; separate empty conversation, offline queue and refused service without overloading `Success`.
