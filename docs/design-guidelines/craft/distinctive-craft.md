# Distinctive app craft

## Decide what the app should feel like by deciding what it does well

An app has a personality when its priorities are recognizable even without its logo. Write one promise in user terms (e.g. “tell me what changed before I run to the wrong gate”), name the ordinary loop, and identify the one moment that proves that promise. Choose one high-attention surface for that moment. Let navigation, money, permissions and errors speak the platform's familiar language. Brand should make the next action easier to identify, not harder to guess.

Google's 2025 Expressive research reports some key elements were located up to four times faster in tested expressive designs. That is evidence for purposeful contrast in those tasks, not a license to turn up the amplitude across an app. Use contrast of size, containment and shape to distinguish importance; if everything is large, bright, moving and contained, the screen has no order. Apple's Liquid Glass is a functional layer above content, not a filter to apply to every content card. Both approaches can support a quiet or a bold identity, depending on what sits in the foreground.

**For lmthing:** A Director brief can describe a single quality, a main user loop, a focal object and an earned signature cue (e.g. route handoff, not every map tick). Choose an archetype and pack pieces before picking a preset. Use `Hero`, `Photo`, `Card`, `Progress`, `Timeline`, `Success`, and `Route` where they already fit; never choose `Canvas` merely to get a custom border. Gap: the catalog doesn't currently enforce an app-level recurrence/budget for a recognizable visual motif across screens plus its calmer error treatment.

## Build a small visual vocabulary and use it repeatedly

- **Theme and surface.** Pick one deliberate accent and repeat it on primary action and selection. Status colors keep their meaning; a brand accent doesn't replace error or verified status. Test contrast against each surface and actual imagery, in dark and light mode. On iOS let the system provide its adaptive material for navigation and adjacent controls; on custom web surfaces favor an opaque or sufficiently strong scrim when blur or contrast fails. Don't simulate a system material in a dense content grid.
- **Typography.** On a 390-wide kit screen, the 20-unit gutters leave 350 content units. Use the kit's 26/32 title or 34/40 display once when warranted, 16/22 body for instructions, and 13/18 only for supporting metadata; 40/44 tabular numbers are for the main numeric read. At most three type sizes per component under the visual rubric. Make type personality a title face or weight, not a hard-to-read body style. Restrict long titles by layout and content priority, not by disabling text scaling. Test larger system type on native and enlarged/reflowed text on web; don't hardcode a one-line card that can't grow.
- **Shape and rhythm.** Use 12–16 inside an ordinary card, 8 between tightly related lines/controls where targets allow, and 24–32 between groups as starting token steps. Keep a repeated alignment edge. A heavy, soft panel can be memorable when only one gets that treatment. Concentric corners help nested controls look related; don't force every tile or toolbar into a capsule. On expressive Material surfaces, a shape morph can emphasize a state change, but its semantic meaning still comes from label and state, not from a shape alone.
- **Illustration.** Select two or three reusable constraints (uniform perspective, line thickness, limited color or silhouette). Keep content photographs distinct from decorative art, with aspect-ratio slots and predictable crops. An optional centered empty-state illustration around 64–96 logical units can leave more room for the action than an oversized mural. Leave decorative art out of the accessible name; give functional art a meaningful equivalent. Supply vector art where suitable, high-density assets on native and responsive sources with reserved dimensions on web.
- **Iconography.** Use familiar icon metaphors and consistent stroke, weight and optical alignment. An ordinary row icon can be 20; a prominent control glyph 24; the hit area remains at least 44 pt iOS, 48 dp Android, or the kit's 44 CSS px on web. Pair ambiguous symbols with text, and distinguish regular/selected with state plus shape or text, not tinted strokes alone. Put proprietary forms in an illustration or product mark instead of the delete/back/send vocabulary.

**For lmthing:** `KitCard`, `KitText`, `KitPhoto`, `KitAction`, theme roles and the catalog icon vocabulary are the primitives; existing `calm`, `bold`, `editorial`, `fresh`, and `playful` presets are closed starting points. Do not pass a font, decorative color, asset geometry or radius through Writer props. Add a composition fixture that contrasts two app identities without comparing different amounts of content. The visual rubric already tests hierarchy, accent usage and variants at 390; future checks should include dark mode, higher text scale, and whether the chosen art changes meaningfully with state.

## Write the voice through the quiet states

Choose everyday nouns and verb-first actions. Keep the terms stable across discovery, detail, receipt and errors. Voice is the recognizable style (e.g. precise and warm); tone changes with stakes (neutral about a rejected payment, encouraging about a milestone). Avoid “Oops!” when the person needs to know whether they were charged, and avoid praise that makes rest or opting out sound like failure.

| State                       | Anatomy and sample copy                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First use                   | Show the missing object, one value statement and one next action. “No walks scheduled. Add a walk to see it here.”                                                                 |
| Filtered to zero            | Keep the query or active chips; say why the list has no rows. “No walks match these filters.” Offer “Clear filters,” not “Get started.”                                            |
| Done for now                | Signal completion rather than missing setup. “You're caught up. New requests will appear here.” Skip a forced CTA.                                                                 |
| Paused, denied or offline   | Explain the actual limitation, keep usable content and give the safe next step. “Showing the last update from 10:42. Reconnect to refresh.” Do not say “Nothing here” on failures. |
| Confirmed and consequential | State amount/object, destination and next action. “Transfer sent” only once sent; “Payment processing” while unresolved, without a cheery visual contradiction.                    |

At 390, center a short standalone empty or success block as a unit (illustration, heading, one explanation and one action); keep a contextual empty directly under the title and filters. Allow 16–24 between groups, not a vast blank box. Keep the action text visible and reachable with the keyboard, large text and safe areas. Review a screenshot with brand assets removed: the hierarchy and recovery must still work. Review it again with content held constant: if only the hue changed, the app probably isn't distinct yet.

**For lmthing:** `Empty`, `Note`, `Banner`, `Success`, `HandoffReceipt`, and conventionally formatted `List` empty states are the toolbox. Built-in strings belong in the component or pack's `lang/en` fragments, authored text in data; reuse the same words in the reader. Gap: make first-use, filtered-empty, caught-up and stale fixtures distinct in recipes rather than requiring a new component for each emotional tone.
