# What Apple Design Award winners do

Apple's winners and developer case studies describe what stood out; they are not controlled usability studies or requirements. The transferable question is **which interaction makes the core task easier to understand or more satisfying?** At a 390-wide preview, prototype the actual loop from entry to result and repeat it. Don't try to match award screens by adding glass, particles, or an unfamiliar button set.

| Case                                                                             | What the source describes                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Transferable direction                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CapWords** (2025, Delight and Fun)                                             | Capturing a real object turns it into an interactive sticker for learning; flash-card transitions add related real-world sound.                                                                                                            | Animate the same object's change of role, not a generic celebration. For a capture-to-record journey, keep the captured subject apparent through the transition and make the resulting label usable immediately. Provide a still/silent equivalent. |
| **Art of Fauna** (2025, Inclusivity)                                             | Each puzzle works through either the picture or its rearrangeable text description. VoiceOver, theme options, and phobia settings are part of the experience.                                                                              | Give a spatial activity a fully capable text/list alternative with the same goal, not merely a description of why it isn't playable. Test the alternative end to end.                                                                               |
| **Speechify** (2025, Inclusivity) and **Guitar Wiz** (2026, Inclusivity)         | Speechify makes many formats listenable and supports large text and VoiceOver. Guitar Wiz explains finger placement and tuning aloud and swaps chord diagrams to a high-contrast rendering.                                                | Treat audio and sight as parallel ways to complete a task. Invest in semantic structure and an alternate rendering for the actual data object rather than hoping system contrast alone will fix a dense graphic.                                    |
| **Play** (2025, Innovation) and **Taobao** (2025, Interaction)                   | Play keeps a prototyping tool approachable and aligned with its SwiftUI output. Taobao uses carefully controlled 3D placement, scale, and side-by-side product comparison on Vision Pro.                                                   | Match preview semantics to real behavior, and make spatial presentation earn its place through a task like comparison. Don't transplant headset gestures to a narrow phone: use stable object identity, a reachable selector and a text comparison. |
| **Watch Duty** (2025, Social Impact)                                             | Clear, timely wildfire information includes perimeters, conditions and evacuation orders.                                                                                                                                                  | In high-stakes views trust and freshness are the differentiators. Prioritize the actionable fact, source/time, and graceful stale behavior over ornamental motion.                                                                                  |
| **grug** (2026, Delight and Fun)                                                 | The team designed a shared hand-drawn type system, matching copy and a stroke-based reveal, and omitted unnecessary account and sync flows.                                                                                                | Make personality coherent across voice, drawing, and one reveal. Keep the main job fast and recognizable. For daily use, let someone skip any long reveal; don't apply novelty to critical or dense controls.                                       |
| **Is This Seat Taken?** (2026, Delight and Fun)                                  | Playtesters wanted to sort characters, not drive the bus; the team cut driving, kept a clear objective, and reserved playful responses for optional discoveries.                                                                           | Simplify the primary interaction before polishing secondary touches. Add delight around a clear task, not a step someone must perform to get to it.                                                                                                 |
| **Moonlitt** (2026, Interaction) and **Tide Guide** (2026, Visuals and Graphics) | Moonlitt hides complex astronomical logic behind easy onboarding, content-forward glass, and focused exploration. Tide Guide pairs readable full-screen charts, context-responsive color, custom animation, and useful glanceable widgets. | Give one important value top billing, layer access to deeper data, and unify chrome and motion around that hierarchy. On the web reproduce the hierarchy and interaction, not the native material or widget capability.                             |

## For lmthing

Pick one repeatable signature tied to the job: capture → new record (`Camera`/`Photo`), peer comparison (`Gallery`/`List` or commerce), progress acknowledged (`Progress`/`Success`), or an interpretable trend (weather/data with `Bars`/`Sparkline`). Use the Spec's closed themes, signatures, variant axes and archetypes to keep destinations and recovery consistent while type, art and a meaningful transition provide character. Test the same job with motion off, with the keyboard, and on a second preset before claiming distinctiveness. Carry the visual rubric beyond screenshots with a short clip or frames of the core loop plus its interruption. Gap: a reviewed signature library could describe which job each sequence serves, when it must not run, and what the equally useful fallback is.
