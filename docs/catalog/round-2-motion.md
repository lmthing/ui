# Round 2 motion contracts

These are component-local contracts. A linked destination owns its screen transition, never the source row. A cut means the displayed and readable states commit together with no outgoing control left interactive. There is no animation at rest.

| Contract | Enter | Change | Feedback | Exit and interruption | Reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work queue | Cut to complete queue or skeleton. | Keep the record and action in the row; status refresh cuts to the new badge. | Kit action fast press effect; pressing opens review, not a fabricated decision. | Cut on navigation; Back restores the initiating row. Refresh/Back never waits for effects. | Cut; same status and target. |
| Decision review | Cut to evidence, line items and decision controls. | Fixed subject and amount; reveal the reason field locally or show the acknowledged decision in its place. | Kit action fast press effect; only host acknowledgement marks a real decision settled. | No animated exit; cancel drops pending interaction and Back restores origin focus. | Cut; no spatial reveal. |
| Record timeline | Cut to the current state and history. | Keep record and timestamps anchored; cut to the summary or detail permitted by the caller. | No cue for passive reading. | Cut between read surfaces; no ghost history or extra action. | Identical cut. |
| Slot agenda | Cut to date and rows, with same loading rail. | Keep time and session anchored when staff or capacity changes. | Fast kit press feedback only on linked rows. | Cut to the destination; Back restores the schedule. | Cut; no moving status rails. |
| Rollup rows | Cut to the reporting scope and exact values. | Keep metric, unit and entity aligned; cut to replacement values rather than count from zero. | Kit press feedback on linked rows only. | Cut; no stale numbers displayed during interrupted navigation. | Identical numbers and cut. |
| Success | First frame is the real outcome, never a delayed reveal. | Replace outcome locally, keeping the record and its time visible. | Kit press effect on real actions; no celebration on pending. | Toast dismiss removes its targets immediately; durable results do not auto-dismiss. | Cut; no-scale result. |
| Key/value facts | Cut to the facts or figure skeleton. | A long value uses the same row address but wraps below its label; update a status in place. | Only existing reveal/copy provides feedback. | Cut; credential visibility follows the shared grant. | Identical cut. |
| Attachments | Cut to current attachment state and available picker. | The same picker address persists across waiting, attached, failed and removed. | Immediate kit press effect, then actual host status; no invented file or progress. | Cancel or removal revokes the current attachment and removes its action when unavailable. | Cut; no simulated upload animation. |
| Checklist | Show initial boxes and exact count at once. | Keep all rows in place; update one box and the aggregate on the same event. | Existing `check_burst` signature only for a meaningful cue, not each remount; immediate persistent checked state. | Another tap reverses the same item with no queued state; focus stays on the checkbox. | Keep the checked mark and count; no burst travel. |
| Empty | Cut to the reason and any valid recovery. | Keep message position stable until replaced by actual content. | Retry uses kit press effect only for retryable failures. | Cut; remove the retry target when no longer applicable. | Identical cut. |
