# Pack benchmark: shipped patterns and gaps

This is a desk review, not a claim of hands-on testing in the reference apps. The comparisons use
documented or familiar product patterns, not copied screens or copy. Apple HIG feedback puts status
next to its subject and distinguishes completion from an in-progress action. Material 3 button hierarchy
gives the next meaningful action priority, and its list guidance gives rows one consistent reading order.

## Priority from test evidence

The rank counts **distinct app ideas in the two tester rounds** where the job appeared, not the number
of retries or a claim about which component the Writer selected. A job can count toward multiple packs.
Ties put a missing or false result ahead of presentation polish. Rows with no clear signal are unranked.
The Quick/H4 captures discussed in the follow-up reports corroborate failure modes, not prevalence:
the dog-walk capture had an unbindable typed Date where the job asked for an available choice, and the
H4 dispatcher and invitation examples lost their cross-role destination. Neither is proof of a kit bug.

| Rank | Pack | Ideas with a relevant reported gap | Recurring need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Booking | 11: dog walking, dental, coworking, tutoring, gym, event venue, veterinary, cleaning, restaurant, yoga, salon | Show real choices first. Once full, wait, review an offer, then confirm. Do not treat a request as booked. |
| 2 | Requests | 9: book swap, coworking, property management, bicycle repair, veterinary, logistics, repair shop, housing co-op, field service | Keep the same reference, state, next owner and next action across the sender and receiver. |
| 3 | Customers | 7: dental, small bakery, event venue, veterinary, cleaning, repair shop, freelance agency | Separate inquiry, proposal, agreement, client, invoice and payment. Preserve identity through the transition. |
| 4 | Team | 6: event venue, school, hospital ward, yoga, clinic network, expense approvals | Show the recipient's pending work or next shift without implying permissions or completing it for them. |
| 5 | Events | 5: youth football league, event venue, wedding, theatre, yoga | Show capacity and attendee state in the right role. Keep the same signup and check-in record. |
| 6 | Money | 4: tutoring, coworking, household budget, expense approvals | Lead with the payable amount or remaining budget, and distinguish pending from settled. |
| 7 | Commerce | 3: small bakery, food marketplace, marketplace | Preserve line items and the order state through checkout, fulfillment and receipt. |
| 8 | Other observed domains | 1–2 each: community, health, home, inventory, kitchen, learning, places, projects, property and sports | See the domain gaps below. There was not enough evidence to put one ahead of the others. |

## Component and flow gaps

The rows cover the 27 pack folders with catalog components on this branch. “Candidate” means it needs
its own contract or composition check, not that the existing component necessarily has a defect.

| Pack | Three reference apps and their patterns | Gap and next screen or flow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking | Mindbody keeps class capacity and waitlist policy with the schedule. Momence distinguishes automatic enrollment from an offered spot. ClassPass explains that a spot can disappear between schedule and reservation. | `SlotAgenda` fills the named-session agenda gap without replacing `Slots` for interchangeable start times. `Waitlist` now shows an offered spot, explicitly separate from a booking. `class_waitlist` plays open, full, expired and booked paths. |
| Requests | Zendesk puts status and assignment on the ticket. Jira Service Management separates request intake, queue, and transitions. Jobber lets a client request work and later see the appointment. | `RequestQueue` and `WorkOrder` do not supply the contract for a requester-to-assignee receipt. `assign_work_order` guides triage, assignment, and closure without inventing a second `HandoffReceipt`. |
| Customers | Pipedrive separates the unqualified Leads Inbox from the deal pipeline. HoneyBook tracks inquiry, follow-up and proposal stages before booked work. Jobber separates client approval, visit and invoice. | `FollowUpQueue` is a due-work list, not a lead pipeline. `ClientCard` is a client, not a prospect. `lead_to_client` adds a proposal hand-off with accept and decline branches. Candidate: read-only pipeline row with next owner and next follow-up. |
| Team | When I Work separates personal shifts from the whole schedule. Deputy calls out open shifts and attendance. Slack keeps asynchronous updates separate from action-required items. | `Shift`, `OpenShift` and `Handover` cover separate records. Missing composition for an owner to offer a shift, a worker to accept, and both to see it assigned. |
| Events | Eventbrite separates orders, ticket issue and organiser check-in. Partiful surfaces response status and invites. Zola groups guests and wedding tasks. | `RSVP`, `Ticket` and `Door` exist, but group response, waitlisted attendees and already-checked-in need distinct states. Candidate: invite to RSVP to arrival. |
| Money | YNAB selects the category before or alongside amount entry. Splitwise shows balances separately from settlements. Monzo places status on the individual transaction. | `AmountPad` and `Transactions` need a composition where a category, currency unit, resulting balance and reversed or pending state agree. Candidate: enter spend to reconciled budget. |
| Commerce | Shopify Shop tracks fulfillment after checkout. Etsy carries variant selection and seller context into purchase. Amazon groups shipments under the same order. | `OrderSummary` and `OrderStatus` should share order reference and promised delivery. Missing partial fulfillment, failed payment and retry in the same journey. |
| Community | Meetup shows RSVP and attendee limits. Discord separates member action from moderation. Nextdoor organizes nearby posts and replies. | `Vote` and `Minutes` do not yet connect a proposal, eligibility, a recorded vote and the closed decision. Candidate: decision vote to minutes. |
| Health | Apple Fitness emphasizes today's progress. Strava separates workout capture from the activity summary. Fitbit gives a trend over an explicit reporting period. | `HabitGrid`, `Workout` and `ActivityRings` need explicit empty versus missed-day semantics and a visible result after logging. Candidate: plan, log, then see week progress. |
| Home | Tody orders recurring chores by what is due. Sweepy assigns household work and tracks completion. Cozi separates family calendar scope from the shared list. | `Chores` and `Agenda` need a shared task identity between one person's completion and another's review. Separate nothing due from nothing assigned. |
| Inventory | Sortly makes the location path visible. Libib separates collection browse and lending. Itemtopia carries documents and warranty facts with the item. | `Things`, `Storage` and `Loans` need an item-level availability or overdue state that follows the same record to return. Candidate: borrow and check in. |
| Kitchen | Paprika connects recipes, meal plans and shopping. Mealime builds a grouped shopping list from the plan. AnyList retains checked items as list state. | `MealPlan`, `GroceryList` and `Pantry` do not state how existing stock reduces what to buy. Candidate: plan to list with substitutes and completion. |
| Learning | Google Classroom separates assignment, submission and feedback. Quizlet keeps study progress distinct from mastery. Duolingo shows the next lesson and its prerequisite. | `Assignments` and `Gradebook` need one consistent submitted, returned, or resubmit trail across learner and reviewer. Do not collapse submitted into graded. |
| Places | Google Maps combines map and place results. Citymapper shows live departures and changes to a route. Uber Eats tracks fulfillment against an arrival estimate. | `MapList` and `DeliveryTracker` need a clearly scoped map alternative, last updated time, and an ETA unavailable state. Candidate: track and report a delivery exception. |
| Projects | Asana gives tasks an owner and dates and separates task detail. Linear places status and activity on the issue. Notion switches views over the same records. | `Tasks`, `Gantt` and `Milestones` should preserve links and show blocked versus overdue. Candidate: assign, update, then review milestone. |
| Property | Buildium separates resident requests from property operations. AppFolio gives tenants a place to check maintenance status and payments. TurboTenant keeps application screening separate from a signed lease. | `Units`, `RentRoll` and `Inspections` need unit and resident scope carried into the decision, not one status field shared across jobs. Candidate: tenant issue to manager assignment. |
| Sports | TeamSnap groups schedule and lineup by team. GameChanger tracks the score and play history. ESPN keeps fixture status separate from the final box score. | `Fixtures` and `Scoreboard` need postponed, submitted and verified states that do not turn a referee report into the public score prematurely. |
| Books | QuickBooks separates reconciliation from reports. Xero keeps the status and history on an invoice. Wave distinguishes a draft transaction from recorded payment. | `Ledger`, `Expenses` and `ProfitLoss` need source reconciliation with an explicit period and excluded or unmatched rows. |
| Data | Airtable offers separate views of the same table. Tableau shows the scale, labels and filters tied to a chart. Looker Studio visibly changes the date and filter scope. | `Table`, `SavedViews` and `LineChart` need stale/no match/filtered states that explain the denominator. Candidate: inspect and drill into a filtered finding. |
| Hiring | LinkedIn distinguishes a saved job and a submitted application. Greenhouse uses a stage, assigned reviewer and candidate history. Indeed shows status from the applicant side. | `Applications` and `ApplicantPipeline` need a submitted-to-decision route with candidate-visible states kept separate from private review notes. |
| Invest | Fidelity distinguishes holdings, pending orders and history. Robinhood previews the trade before submission. Vanguard shows account performance over selectable periods. | `Holdings`, `InvestmentActivity` and `Performance` need a stated pricing time and executed versus queued order states; no new trade mechanics proposed. |
| Media | Spotify separates the Now Playing view and queue. Apple Podcasts exposes episode chapters and resume progress. Audible carries place in a book into the next session. | `MiniPlayer`, `ChapterList` and `Transcript` need one consistent playhead and a clear unavailable-media or transcript-not-ready state. |
| Mind | Headspace separates a session selection from its player. Daylio uses quick entry before longer reflection. Calm keeps optional audio controls near the session. | `Feelings`, `Entries` and `Patterns` should distinguish a skipped entry from no data and avoid treating a trend as a diagnosis. Candidate: log and reflect. |
| Paperwork | Adobe Scan separates capture, cleanup and export. DocuSign shows pending signatures and signed evidence. Dropbox Sign distinguishes the person who must act from a completed document. | `ScanBatch`, `Documents` and `Signature` need capture-quality and missing-signer states, with continuity from draft to final copy. |
| Play | Kahoot separates lobby, question and round result. Jackbox answers privately before displaying the group result. Board Game Arena keeps turn ownership visible across the board. | `BuzzerPad`, `Trivia` and `TurnOrder` need a clear not-your-turn state and an intentional score reset; neither a disabled button nor a decorative badge is enough. |
| Social | Instagram distinguishes draft, published post and Story visibility. Reddit shows thread structure and moderation states. Discord keeps typing, sending and error states near the composer. | `Post`, `Comments` and `Feed` need retry and delivery feedback, an identified audience, and a missing/deleted thread state. |
| Trips | TripIt groups bookings into a timeline. Flighty keeps last update and status beside the flight. Airbnb carries the reservation date and status into change/cancellation. | `Itinerary`, `FlightStatus` and `Reservation` need a linked disruption or rebooking path that keeps the original record and distinguishes it from a replacement. |
