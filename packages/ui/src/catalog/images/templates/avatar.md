# Prompt

Make a fictional, clearly illustrated head-and-shoulders portrait, never a real or photorealistic person. Give it a distinct contour and readable facial expression at avatar size.

No text, numerals, lettering, watermarks, logos or real brands. No real people or recognizable likenesses. No hands unless essential to the subject.

# Reject if

- Any text, logo, watermark, real brand, recognizable real person or unnecessary hand.
- The subject or requested art treatment is not recognizable.
- The face is photographic, resembles a known person, is cut by a circular crop or cannot be read at thumbnail size.
