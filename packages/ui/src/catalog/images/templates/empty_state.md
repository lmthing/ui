# Prompt

Show a quiet visual cue for what belongs here, as a small still life with one to three objects and generous open ground. The scene should feel expectant rather than sad.

Show the objects themselves, unpackaged and unmarked. No packets, wrappers, tags, labels or stamped marks.

No text, numerals, lettering, watermarks, logos or real brands. No real people or recognizable likenesses. No hands unless essential to the subject.

# Reject if

- Any text, logo, watermark, real brand, recognizable real person or unnecessary hand.
- The subject or requested art treatment is not recognizable.
- There is packaging, or the image reads as a completed collection, a warning or a busy ornamental scene.
