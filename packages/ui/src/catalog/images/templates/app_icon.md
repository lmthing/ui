# Prompt

Make a simple symbolic distillation of the subject, not an identity mark for any real brand. Fill the ground to the corners; do not draw the device mask.

No text, numerals, lettering, watermarks, logos or real brands. No real people or recognizable likenesses. No hands unless essential to the subject.

# Reject if

- Any text, logo, watermark, real brand, recognizable real person or unnecessary hand.
- The subject or requested art treatment is not recognizable.
- The motif cannot be identified at a glance at launcher size; the background includes a fake tile, lettering or an existing mark.
