# Prompt

Create a single striking visual for a fictional event or offering. Use a strong scale relationship and carry the art across the frame, with no graphic layout, titles or credits.

No text, numerals, lettering, watermarks, logos or real brands. No real people or recognizable likenesses. No hands unless essential to the subject.

# Reject if

- Any text, logo, watermark, real brand, recognizable real person or unnecessary hand.
- The subject or requested art treatment is not recognizable.
- It looks like an imitation of a known poster, includes lettering or lacks a clear focal subject.
