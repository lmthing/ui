## Purpose

Review a cart, select fulfillment, and place an order before collecting payment.

## Pick

Choose for editing quantities, reviewing price and availability, and selecting pickup or delivery before placing a goods order.

## Avoid

Not for paying an existing balance or following an order that has already been placed.

## Writer

- Hold the same item, quantity, price and reference through review and confirmation. Orders are not payments.
- If an item or price changes, keep the cart and require fresh review. Do not confirm a removed item or silently change the total.
