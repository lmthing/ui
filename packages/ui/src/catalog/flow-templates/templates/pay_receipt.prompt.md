## Purpose

Pay a known amount and find a receipt only after payment succeeds.

## Pick

Choose for payment of a known charge, confirmation and durable proof of payment.

## Avoid

Not for placing an order with delivery tracking or requesting permission to spend.

## Writer

- Show amount, currency and payee before committing. Use a safe method label, never raw credentials.
- Copy the full order label (name and reference if both appear) into order. Keep the payment receipt reference separate.
- A pending or failed payment must not produce a receipt. Retrying should not duplicate a settled charge.
