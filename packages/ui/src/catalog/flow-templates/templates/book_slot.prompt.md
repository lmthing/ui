## Purpose

Reserve an available time and manage the booking without losing its reference.

## Pick

Choose for reserving a specific available time with a confirmation and a way to change or cancel it.

## Avoid

Not for checking in to an existing booking, paying a bill, or scheduling without a reservation.

## Writer

- Show time zone or local time before confirmation, and keep the booking reference on changes.
- Offer reschedule and cancel as separate paths. Keep the old slot until the new time is confirmed.
