## Purpose

Hand physical custody to a named receiver and record whether they took it.

## Pick

Choose when a sender transfers an item, batch or shift kit and a receiver opens it from their queue to acknowledge or decline custody.

## Avoid

Not for approving a request, assigning work, tracking a customer order, or moving money.

## Writer

- Keep the subject and reference identical for both roles. Sent or overdue still means the receiver has not accepted custody.
- Use a specific receiving deadline, pending note and actionable return reason. Never infer receipt from dispatch.
