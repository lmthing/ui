## Purpose

Send an invitation with a defined role and confirm that the recipient joined.

## Pick

Choose when someone grants access by inviting another person who must explicitly accept.

## Avoid

Not for account creation alone or for a person requesting approval for themselves.

## Writer

- Before joining, show who sent the invitation, what the recipient joins and their access.
- Keep pending separate from joined. For an expired or wrong-account invitation, offer a way to get a new invitation.
