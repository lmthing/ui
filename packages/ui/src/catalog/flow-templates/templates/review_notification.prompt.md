## Purpose

Find a new update, open its durable record, and return to the notification inbox.

## Pick

Choose for an in-app inbox of event notifications with unread or read status and a link to the affected record.

## Avoid

Not for sending personal replies, configuring a preference, tracking all stages of an order, or requesting system notification permission.

## Writer

- Show the change first and date it. Keep the notification reference attached to the affected record.
- Keep records accessible when system notifications are denied. Do not claim delivery to the lock screen.
