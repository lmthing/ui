## Purpose

Report a problem, follow the work, and confirm the resolution.

## Pick

Choose when a person reports a problem and another role investigates or fixes it.

## Avoid

Not for granting permission, approving a request, or tracking a shipment.

## Writer

- Give the report a reference and carry it across intake, assignment and resolution.
- If the request includes a location, asset tag or attachment label, copy it to evidence and keep the context step. Otherwise set evidence to "No evidence attached" and omit the context step.
- Tell the reporter what was fixed instead of only showing a closed status.
