## Purpose

Send a message in a conversation and receive the other person's reply.

## Pick

Choose for a two-way thread with a draft, send status, and a reply from the recipient.

## Avoid

Not for inviting someone to join a space, broadcasting without replies, or transferring custody.

## Writer

- Preserve the exact message and thread in both roles. Sent, delivered and read are separate facts.
- Keep the draft if sending fails. Do not offer Send on an unavailable thread.
