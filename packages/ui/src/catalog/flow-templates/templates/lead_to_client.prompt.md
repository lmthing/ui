## Purpose

Turn an inquiry into a client after the prospect agrees to a proposal.

## Pick

Choose for qualifying incoming leads, offering a named scope and price, handing the same proposal to the prospect, and creating a client after acceptance.

## Avoid

Not for paying an existing invoice, approving an internal request, or editing an existing client.

## Writer

- Use FollowUpQueue for a list of leads needing outreach and ClientCard and ClientHistory for the resulting client. Do not put unqualified inquiries in the active client list.
- Keep the reference and scope across both roles. Distinguish sent, accepted, declined, and client created. Acceptance is not payment.
