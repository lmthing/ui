## Purpose

Reserve a group class directly or join a full class's waitlist and claim an offered spot.

## Pick

Choose for a class schedule where a full session needs a waitlist, a later reviewable spot offer, and an explicit booking result.

## Avoid

Not for a one-to-one appointment with available slots, or attendance for people who already booked.

## Writer

- Keep the class name and local start time through availability, queue, offer, and booking. Use SlotAgenda for named classes, Slots for interchangeable start times, and Waitlist for queue membership or a real spot offer.
- A waitlist position or offer never means booked. Review the spot before confirming, and preserve the expired or unavailable result without implying a reservation.
