## Purpose

Complete a form in clear steps, review it, and keep proof of submission.

## Pick

Choose for a multi-step form that collects different groups of information and reviews them before one submission.

## Avoid

Not for creating an account, editing a profile or one preference, reserving a slot, or making a payment.

## Writer

- Number only the actual steps. Keep completed entries when moving Back or correcting a validation error.
- Show the full draft before sending. Pending is not submitted; acknowledge with the same reference only after acceptance.
