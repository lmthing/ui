## Purpose

Send a request to a decision maker and return the decision, including a rejection reason.

## Pick

Choose when a requester needs explicit approval from another role before going ahead.

## Avoid

Not for inviting someone to join or resolving an issue by doing the work.

## Writer

- Before the decision, show who asked, what they need and why.
- Require a reason before rejection. The requester sees the reason and the same request reference.
