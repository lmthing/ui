## Purpose

The screen's main statement or headline number, including what needs attention today.

## Pick

The focal message of the whole screen: its main headline, photo-led introduction or headline number. A count of things needing attention can be the main headline, not an alert. Not a separate warning above other content (Banner), a supporting sentence (Note), several numbers (Stats) or one sum of money (Amount).

## Writer

- Use style "number" when the title is a headline number. For a requested wide photo, set media to "cover" and give its photo reference; density, emphasis, align and tone stay null unless explicitly requested.
- Put visible subtitles in sub and keep detail null. Only an explicit request to hide the subtitle sets detail to "brief"; keep the subtitle in sub.
