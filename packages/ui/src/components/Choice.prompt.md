## Purpose

Choose among two to five options that each need a sentence to explain.

## Pick

Choosing among a few options that each carry a short explanation, such as a plan, an answer to a question or a way of doing something. Not one-word filters (Chips), a short value inside a form (Field), switching views of one screen (Segmented), or keep-or-skip one at a time (SwipeStack).

## Writer

- Use detail "brief" only to hide secondary option notes, while keeping them in the data.
- To hide the option explanations, keep them in sub and set detail to "brief"; badges and values stay visible.
