## Purpose

Watch a person’s short-lived story and respond to the current slide.

## Pick

Watch one person’s temporary multi-slide story with timed progress, stickers or quick replies. Not a static photo carousel (Gallery), a row of people’s stories (Stories), or a permanent Post.

## Writer

- Each slide needs its own photo and time; current is a zero-based index into slides. For a read-only viewer use detail "brief"; leave detail null otherwise.
- A link sticker can have a goto; a poll sticker can have up to two options. Use null for unrequested variant axes, including aspect.
