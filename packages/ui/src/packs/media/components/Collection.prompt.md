## Purpose

See one show's details, start the whole series and follow new releases.

## Pick

The overview at the top of one show, album, book or course, with artwork, credits, play-all and follow. Not a photo and headline (Hero), a person (Profile), one recording (Player), or owned things (Things).

## Writer

- Count and duration come from Playlist on this screen; never write either value.
- Use detail "brief" only when asked to hide the description. Keep it in about and leave density and aspect null unless explicitly requested.
