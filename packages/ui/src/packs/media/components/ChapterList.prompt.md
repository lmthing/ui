## Purpose

Jump to named chapters of the recording already on this screen.

## Pick

Named timestamps inside the recording already on this screen; choose one to jump to its start. Not playback controls for one recording (Player), separate recordings (Playlist), or recipe steps (CookSteps).

## Writer

- Use only next to a Player on the same screen. Put 2–16 chapter times in strictly increasing order, whole seconds from the start. "current" is a zero-based index.
- Leave density and surface null unless a different spacing or surface is requested.
