## Purpose

See the whole trip day by day, including stops and travel between them.

## Pick

A whole trip grouped into foldable dated days with places, timed stops and travel legs. Not a single day's time order (Timeline), a path through places (Route), or a family week (Agenda).

## Writer

- Use 1–14 dated days and 1–6 stops per day. Give each stop a title and a goto screen id. Only a travel stop may have minutes.
- Leave density and surface null unless requested. To hide travel durations, set only detail to "brief" and keep minutes on travel stops.
