## Purpose

Track who is invited, who is coming and what each party needs.

## Pick

An event's invitation roster with party groups, RSVP statuses, plus-ones and dietary needs. Not a plain roster of people (People), a guest sending their own reply (RSVP), clients (ClientCard) or a squad's availability (Squad).

## Writer

- Set detail to "brief" only when dietary notes should not be displayed. Keep supplied diet in the data. Set density and surface to JSON null unless explicitly requested.
