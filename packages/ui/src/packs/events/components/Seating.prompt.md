## Purpose

Arrange guests at tables and see who still needs a seat.

## Pick

A seating plan of round or long tables with guest initials, available seats and a tray of unseated guests. Not locations on a geographic map (MapPins), an invitation roster (GuestList) or a staff rota (Roster).

## Writer

- Each table's guests array may be empty and cannot exceed seats. Table labels must be distinct; unseated is an array of names, not a number.
