## Purpose

Quickly log a baby's feeds, nappies and sleeps and time an active feed or sleep.

## Pick

Baby feeds, nappies and sleeps with quick tiles, an active timer and today's log. Not timing one thing (Timer), adult habits (HabitGrid), or a general care history (Timeline).

## Writer

- log has 0–12 entries. Write kind, HH:MM time and optional minutes/amount; never time-since-last. running is none or one kind, not a boolean.
- Use detail "brief" only when asked to hide durations and amounts in history. Keep them in log.
