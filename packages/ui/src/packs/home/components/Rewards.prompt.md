## Purpose

Keep children's earned points or pocket money and claim prizes they can afford.

## Pick

Children's points earned and spent on prizes with costs. Not one person's goal (Progress), a business stamp card (Loyalty), or milestone badges (Achievements).

## Writer

- Write stored points and prize costs as nonnegative numbers. Claimability and balances after a claim are computed, never written. money means pocket money; never write a currency.
- Use detail "brief" only when asked to hide the amount still needed for unaffordable prizes. Keep the costs and disabled controls.
