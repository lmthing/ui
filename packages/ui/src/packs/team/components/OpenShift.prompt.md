## Purpose

Pick up an unassigned shift and see when it runs and what it pays.

## Pick

Claim one of several unassigned work shifts with a date, time range, role and optional pay. Not an assigned shift (Shift) or a list of jobs to tick off (Checklist).

## Writer

- Keep pay as an optional numeric amount without a currency. Use detail "brief" only when asked to hide pay, and retain any supplied pay in the data. Leave density and surface null unless requested.
