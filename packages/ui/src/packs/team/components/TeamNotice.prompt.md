## Purpose

Read important workplace notices and acknowledge that you have read them.

## Pick

An official workplace bulletin, policy or staff announcement with author, date, priority and read receipts. Not a shift handover (Handover), a short transient warning (Banner), or a social post (Post).

## Writer

- For a pinned strip that links to the full bulletin, set detail to "brief" and keep the full body and goto.
- Read count is the number who already read it, including the current member if acknowledged is true. Never make it greater than totalMembers.
