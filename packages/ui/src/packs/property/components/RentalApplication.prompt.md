## Purpose

Review prospective tenants, compare stated income to rent, and record a screening decision.

## Pick

Screening prospective tenants for a specific rental unit with move-in, household income against rent, verification checks and a waitlist, approval or decline. Not a static facts card (KeyValue), job recruiting (Candidate), viewing appointments (Viewings), or active tenancies (Units).

## Writer

- Use snake_case application and unit refs, a real YYYY-MM-DD move-in date, and numeric monthly rent and income. Give each check a distinct kind. Do not write household totals or ratios.
- Use detail "brief" for a summary without notes or decision buttons; otherwise leave it null for the full dossier. Leave density and surface null unless requested.
