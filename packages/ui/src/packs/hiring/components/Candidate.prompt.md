## Purpose

Review evidence for one job application and record a human hiring decision.

## Pick

One application with criterion-by-criterion findings and evidence, interview and offer links, and a human decision. Not identity alone (Profile), generic facts (KeyValue), a client summary (ClientCard), or a rating (Scale).

## Writer

- Findings are explicitly recorded by a human. Default to unreviewed when no finding is supplied; never infer qualifications, assign a score or rank candidates.
- For a findings-only view, keep authored evidence in the data and set detail to "brief".
