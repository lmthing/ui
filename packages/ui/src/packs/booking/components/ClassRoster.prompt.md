## Purpose

Check attendees in to a group class and fill newly open places.

## Pick

Take attendance for named attendees at a scheduled group class, admit walk-ins or promote waitlisted guests. Not capacity bars alone (Progress), read-only avatars (People), or staff shifts (Roster).

## Writer

- Give each attendee a unique id. Use an ISO timestamp with timezone such as "2026-11-21T10:00:00-05:00".
- To hide the date, time and payment labels, set only detail to "brief". Keep the timestamp and paid flags in the data.
