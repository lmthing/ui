## Purpose

Name up to three precise feelings by choosing words within a family.

## Pick

Choose a family such as calm or anxious, then toggle up to three precise feeling words. Not one five-point rating (Scale), things that happened (Factors), or physical symptoms (SymptomLog).

## Writer

- Give three to six families, each with three to eight words. Chosen has up to three distinct words from the families; use [] when none are selected.
- Use detail "brief" only when asked to hide the instructions, and keep every family and word.
