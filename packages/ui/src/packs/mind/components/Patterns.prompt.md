## Purpose

Find which logged daily factors tend to go with better or worse moods.

## Pick

Derive findings from a mood journal by comparing days with and without a tagged factor after at least two weeks. Choose this for journal-derived comparisons even when findings also use paired bars. Not plotting values the person already supplied (Bars) or a single time trend (Sparkline).

## Writer

- Use factor labels from the same screen's Factors when available. Only choose labels and minDays; never invent findings or write mood averages or bar values.
- Leave density, surface and detail null unless requested. When asked for finding sentences without comparison charts, set detail to "brief".
