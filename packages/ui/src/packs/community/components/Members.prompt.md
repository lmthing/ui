## Purpose

Keep membership records, voting rights and dues badges together.

## Pick

A group's official membership register with joining or leaving dates, voting rights and dues badges. Not a small directory (People), one person's identity (Profile), business clients (ClientCard) or event guests (GuestList).

## Writer

- Use unique snake_case member ids. Leave ended unset for continuing members; when supplied it must not precede joined. Dues badges are computed by the host.
- For names, primary roles and badges only, write detail "brief" and keep the dates, roles and photo in the data.
