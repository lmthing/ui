## Purpose

Review join requests and flagged content under community rules.

## Pick

Moderate a group's join requests or reported posts and comments: scan evidence, choose a rule reason, and approve, decline or remove. Not expandable notices (List), a member register (Members), repair tickets (RequestTriage) or job candidates (ApplicantPipeline).

## Writer

- Use distinct snake_case item ids, distinct community rules, and an ISO submission timestamp with Z or a numeric offset. Keep screening answers and any stated eligibility in detail. Do not write the pending count.
- Use style "joins" only for pending membership applications without flagged content on screen. Use detail "brief" to hide the extra screening or report detail while preserving it in the data.
