## Purpose

Follow a body measurement's long-term history against a goal across selectable time ranges.

## Pick

Long body-measurement history with a dashed goal reference, latest value, change and month/quarter/year switching. Not today's weight with a tiny recent trend and no goal or range switching (Sparkline), progress toward a total (Progress), a holding's price (PriceChart), or business series (LineChart).

## Writer

- Use detail brief to hide the change since the first reading; keep all measurement points and any supplied goal.
