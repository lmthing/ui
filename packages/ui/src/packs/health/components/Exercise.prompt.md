## Purpose

Learn one exercise's form, muscles and equipment, and recall previous results.

## Pick

A reference for one movement with written form cues, muscles, equipment or past results. Never pick for video playback alone: if the person only asks to play or control an exercise video, choose Player. Not a course lesson (Lesson) or a whole session (Workout).

## Writer

- A supplied photo normally shows in a wide frame. To hide it, set media to none and keep the photo reference.
- For a generated image, use `step_illustration` or `item` in `photo`. Use to show equipment or an exercise setup, not to teach form or imply a medical result. Keep any supplied reference. In an image slot, use a short plain noun phrase from the app’s world for `subject`; no style, camera, palette or layout words.
