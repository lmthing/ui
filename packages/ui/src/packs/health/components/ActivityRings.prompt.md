## Purpose

Track multiple daily goals or a supplied wellness score with its factors.

## Pick

At least two distinct daily goals with their own value and target, or one supplied readiness or bio-age score with three supplied factors, as concentric rings. Not a single step, exercise, water or other goal: choose Progress for any one goal, regardless of health context. Not targets drawn as bars (Progress).

## Writer

- To hide target captions, use detail brief and still write each target in of.
- For a supplied readiness or bio-age score out of 100, use style "score", score {label, value}, and exactly three rings for the supplied factors. Otherwise leave style and score null.
