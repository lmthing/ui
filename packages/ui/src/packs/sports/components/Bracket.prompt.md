## Purpose

Follow knockout ties and see who advances to the next round.

## Pick

A knockout cup as paired teams per round, with winners advancing. Only use it for actual head-to-head ties; a top-scorers ranking is List, even in a cup. Not a league table (Standings) or a sequence of matches (Fixtures).

## Writer

- Write rounds earliest to latest, and ties in bracket order. Do not write winners or connectors. Later rounds use the team names already known to advance; omit scores for ties not played.
- For team names and winner markers without printed scores, write "detail":"brief" and keep the known scores in data. Otherwise write "detail":null.
