## Purpose

See who can play and let the coach pick the side.

## Pick

Players' availability for one upcoming match: yes, maybe or no, an optional reason, and coach selection. Not an ordinary player directory (People), who plays where (Lineup), guest RSVPs (GuestList), or staff shifts (Roster).

## Writer

- Put the current player's own row first; only that row offers a reply. Picked is the coach's selection, separate from the answer. Do not write totals.
- To show replies without reasons, write detail "brief" and still supply any known reasons.
