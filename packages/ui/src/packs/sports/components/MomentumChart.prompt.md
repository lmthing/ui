## Purpose

See which team held the advantage and when the match turned.

## Pick

How momentum or win probability shifted during one match, with match-clock events. Not team totals (MatchStats), a tiny personal trend (Sparkline), or a business trend (LineChart).

## Writer

- Use chronological match order and unique point ids. Win probability is 0–100 with 50 even; momentum is -100–100 with 0 even and positive values favoring home.
- Use detail "brief" only to hide event callouts; keep the supplied events.
