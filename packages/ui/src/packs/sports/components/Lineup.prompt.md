## Purpose

See where the starters play and who is on the bench.

## Pick

Starters arranged in their playing lines on a pitch or grouped by position, with a separate substitutes bench. Not an ordinary list of people (People), player availability (Squad) or a staff rota (Roster).

## Writer

- Write lines from the back of the pitch to the front. Put only starters in lines; put substitutes in subs.
- For the formation without the count beside the title, write "detail":"brief" and keep the lines and subs. Otherwise write "detail":null.
- If any starter or substitute has rating, cards, or goals, you must explicitly write "style":"list". Never write "pitch" or null for those entries. Ratings are 0–10, cards is a list of "yellow" or "red", and goals is an optional nonnegative integer.
