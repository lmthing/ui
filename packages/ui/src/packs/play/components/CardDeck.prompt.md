## Purpose

Draw cards at random without replacement and reshuffle the discards when needed.

## Pick

Randomly draw one to three cards without replacement from a finite deck. Not keep-or-skip sorting (SwipeStack), a spinner with repeats (Spinner), or host-read questions (Trivia).

## Writer

- Write the authored deck and draw size. Never write shuffle order, current draws, or pile counts.
- For a brief deck summary (show how many cards remain and hide the discard count), write {"detail":"brief"}. Null detail keeps both counts visible.
