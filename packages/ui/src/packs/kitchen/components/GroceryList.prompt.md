## Purpose

Buy groceries by aisle and tick off what is in the bag.

## Pick

Shopping for food grouped by aisle, with quantities and optional people who added it. Not general jobs (Checklist), recipe amounts (Ingredients), or an online basket (Cart).

## Writer

- Each aisle has 1–12 items; use 1–8 aisles. got is a stored check state. Do not write totals or computed sorting.
- If asked to hide who added items, set detail to "brief" and keep the author data. Otherwise leave detail null.
