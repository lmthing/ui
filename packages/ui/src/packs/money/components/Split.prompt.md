## Purpose

Work out who owes whom after sharing expenses.

## Pick

Shared costs, each person's balance and the payments to settle the group. Not one sum owed (Amount), personal bills (Bills), client payments (PaymentStatus), or fixed debt repayment (Loan, Payoff).

## Writer

- For each expense write by (payer index) and split: "equal" or "shares". Both use one share per person. Never write derived totals. To hide avatars alone, use lead none and keep photos.
